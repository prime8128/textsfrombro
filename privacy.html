Privacy
Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Texts From Bro, operated by Sourcedragon LLC, provides personal reminder and habit text messages that users set for themselves.

Information we collect

Your mobile phone number, the reminders and schedule you create, and the messages you exchange with Bro. If you subscribe, we also receive your email address and the last four digits and brand of your payment card from our payment processor.

We never see or store your full card number. Payment details are entered directly with Stripe and handled by them.

How we use it

To send you the reminder text messages you asked for, to reply when you text us, and to manage your account and billing. No mobile information or messaging consent will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell or rent your mobile phone number or opt-in data to anyone.

Service providers

We use a small number of companies to operate the service. They process your information only to provide their service to us, and not for their own marketing:

Twilio delivers text messages to and from your phone.
Anthropic generates Bro's replies. The text of your messages and reminders is sent to Anthropic's API to produce a response.
Stripe processes payments and stores your payment method.
Resend sends account emails.
Supabase and Fly.io host our database and application.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and reminders for as long as you use the service. If you text Bro but never subscribe, we delete your information after 30 days of inactivity. If you cancel a paid subscription, we keep your account so you can return; email us if you would like it deleted.

Your choices

Reply STOP to stop all messages. Email hello@textsfrombro.com to ask what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted.

Children

Texts From Bro is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Message frequency

Varies based on the reminders you set (typically a few messages per day).

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help. Stopping messages does not cancel a paid subscription — cancel from your account billing page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top reflects the most recent change.
